What we work on

研究方向

Our work sits at the intersection of machine learning systems, computer networking, and security. We especially welcome people who want to explore, among other topics:

  1. ML for Systems, Systems for ML: foundational ML systems and techniques for the networked world.
  2. Network Security and Privacy: our tools applied to critical security and privacy challenges.
  3. Cyber-physical Intelligence: better interactions with the cyber-physical world.
  4. Your own research interests.

We aim for top venues in networking and measurement (SIGCOMM, NSDI, CoNEXT, SIGMETRICS, IMC), security (IEEE S&P, USENIX Security, CCS, NDSS), and ubiquitous and mobile computing (UbiComp, MobiCom, MobiSys, SenSys). Recent lab papers have also appeared at MICRO, VLDB, and SoCC.

实验室的研究位于机器学习系统、计算机网络与安全的交叉，希望有能力和兴趣探索（包括但不限于）以下方向的同学/同事：

  1. ML for Systems, Systems for ML：为网络世界构建基础的机器学习系统与技术。
  2. Network Security and Privacy：用我们的工具解决关键的安全与隐私问题。
  3. Cyber-physical Intelligence：改进人与信息物理世界的交互。
  4. 你自己感兴趣的方向。

实验室的目标会议包括但不限于：网络及测量（SIGCOMM / NSDI / CoNEXT / SIGMETRICS / IMC），安全（S&P / USENIX Security / CCS / NDSS），普适计算或移动计算（UbiComp / MobiCom / MobiSys / SenSys）。近期论文也发表于 MICRO、VLDB、SoCC 等会议。

What we offer

我能提供什么支持

A Ph.D. is not an easy journey, but for people who seek intellectual freedom it often pays off in the end. In our lab we call each other by first name: I am your advisor, and also your colleague, a fellow scholar working alongside you toward your academic goals.

Shinan Liu, Lab Director
  • Guidance that grows with you. Early on, I teach the research skills you need hands-on and provide ideas, guidance, and resources. Later in your Ph.D., you get room to explore on your own.
  • A respectful lab. My own Ph.D. advisor, Nick Feamster, led with integrity, a light touch, and deep respect for students. I am building the same kind of lab, so you can focus on research rather than anything else.

HK$19,135 a month for Ph.D. and MPhil students, rising to HK$19,655 once a Ph.D. candidature is confirmed (rates from 1 September 2026).
Hong Kong PhD Fellowship
HK$344,400 a year (about HK$28,700 a month) plus HK$14,400 a year for conference and research travel, for up to three years (2026/27 rates).
HKU Presidential PhD Scholarship
HKPF awardees at HKU automatically receive this package: HK$28,700 a month, a cash award of HK$40,000 in year 1 and HK$20,000 a year after that, HK$14,400 a year for travel, tuition waived for the normative study period, and a guaranteed hall place in year 1. HKU also offers it to selected full-time Ph.D. entrants, worth up to about HK$1.7 million in total.
Postdocs and on-site RAs
Paid according to HKU standards.

Rates are adjusted every year. See the HKU Graduate School and the Research Grants Council for current details.

About the advisor

导师介绍

Shinan Liu is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Data and Systems Engineering at The University of Hong Kong and directs NAISS Lab. Dr. Liu received a Ph.D. and M.S. in Computer Science from The University of Chicago, advised by Prof. Nick Feamster (ACM Fellow), worked closely with Prof. Vyas Sekar at Carnegie Mellon University, and holds a B.Eng. (Honors) from the Yingcai Honors College of UESTC.

Recognitions include the 2025 ML and Systems Rising Stars, the Carnegie Bosch Fellowship, the ACTION AI Fellowship, and the Daniels Fellowship. Dr. Liu previously headed the student advisory council of the NSF ACTION AI Institute.

The research has appeared at USENIX Security, NDSS, NSDI, SIGMETRICS, CoNEXT, MICRO, and UbiComp. LEAF was deployed in Verizon's cellular network, and the GPS spoofing work was featured in Forbes, The Wall Street Journal, and ACM TechNews.

Dr. Liu serves on the program committees of SIGCOMM, CCS, USENIX Security, IMC, SIGMETRICS, PETS, and NSDI, and reviews for NeurIPS, KDD, UbiComp/IMWUT, USENIX ATC, IEEE INFOCOM, IEEE TDSC, IEEE TIFS, and IEEE IoTJ.

HKU and DASE

学校及院系介绍

The University of Hong Kong is the oldest institution of higher learning in Hong Kong and an international research university, ranked 11th in the 2026 QS World University Rankings. The main campus sits on a hillside in the west of Hong Kong Island overlooking Victoria Harbour, a short MTR, bus, or tram ride from Central and Admiralty, with dining, shops, and sports facilities close by.

The Department of Data and Systems Engineering (DASE) was founded in 1973 as the Department of Industrial Engineering and took its current name in 2024. About 3,000 students have completed its undergraduate, master's, and doctoral programmes. The department follows a computing-in-practice philosophy that joins systems modelling, data-driven optimization, and real-world deployment. HKU ranks 18th in the world and first in Hong Kong for data science in the 2026 QS World University Rankings by Subject.

香港大学是香港历史最悠久的高等学府，也是一所国际化程度较高的研究型大学，在2026年QS世界大学排名中位列第11位。主校区位于港岛西区，依山而建，俯瞰维多利亚港，可通过港铁、巴士、电车快速到达中环、金钟等主要商业区，周边餐饮、超市、书店、运动设施一应俱全。

数据与系统工程系（DASE）创立于1973年，前身为工业工程系，2024年更名为数据与系统工程系，至今已有约3,000名学生完成本科、硕士及博士学位课程。DASE秉持“计算应用于实践”（computing-in-practice）的理念，重视跨学科系统建模、数据驱动优化和实际部署能力。在2026年QS世界大学学科排名中，港大数据科学位列全球第18、香港第一。
